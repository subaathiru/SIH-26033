## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriLink AI: Smart Farm-to-Consumer Marketplace

AgriLink AI is a digital platform that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, restaurants, and bulk buyers. The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by enabling farmers to list their produce, set prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.

The solution integrates AI-driven demand forecasting to predict market requirements based on historical sales, seasonal trends, weather conditions, and consumer demand. A smart logistics system optimizes delivery routes and groups shipments to reduce transportation costs and delivery time.

Key features include:

Direct farmer-to-consumer marketplace.
AI-based demand prediction.
Smart price recommendation system.
Real-time inventory tracking.
Integrated digital payments.
Logistics and delivery management.
Multilingual mobile application for farmers.

This solution ensures fair pricing, transparency, and increased profitability for farmers while providing fresh produce at affordable prices to consumers.

Methodology
Farmer registers and uploads available produce.
Consumers and buyers browse products and place orders.
AI predicts demand and recommends optimal pricing.
Orders are consolidated for efficient transportation.
Route optimization identifies the shortest and most cost-effective delivery path.
Digital payment is processed securely.
Produce is delivered directly to buyers.

Workflow:

Farmer/FPO → Product Listing → AI Demand Forecasting → Buyer Order → Route Optimization → Payment Processing → Delivery → Feedback & Analytics
## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility Analysis
High smartphone and internet penetration in rural India supports digital adoption.
Existing payment systems like UPI enable seamless transactions.
Cloud-based infrastructure reduces deployment and maintenance costs.
AI models can be trained using publicly available agricultural and market datasets.
Potential Challenges
Limited digital literacy among some farmers.
Logistics management in remote areas.
Data accuracy for forecasting models.
Initial user acquisition and trust-building.
Risk Mitigation Strategies
Develop a simple multilingual user interface.
Partner with local FPOs and logistics providers.
Provide training and onboarding programs for farmers.
Continuously improve AI models using real-time market data.
Implement secure payment and verification mechanisms.
